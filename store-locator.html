Good to know

Before you visit

Does every store carry every La Femme dress?

No. Selection varies by retailer, so call ahead with the style number, color, and size you would like to try.

Can a retailer help me order a specific style?

Many authorized retailers can check availability or explain special-order options. Contact the store directly for timing.

How do I know a retailer is authorized?

Use the retailers listed in this directory or contact La Femme for verification before purchasing.
